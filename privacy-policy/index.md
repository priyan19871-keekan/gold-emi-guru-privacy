# Privacy Policy

**App:** Gold Loan EMI Guru  
**Effective date:** October 29, 2026  
**Contact:** [kstechsoftwaresolutions@gmail.com](mailto:kstechsoftwaresolutions@gmail.com)

Gold Loan EMI Guru ("the app") is a loan calculation and planning app. This
policy describes information handled by the app and choices available to you.

## Information you enter

The app processes loan details you enter, which may include a loan name,
amount, interest rate, term, dates, and part payments. Calculations run on your
device. Details in the current form are held by the app while it is in use.

If you choose **Save Loan**, the selected scenario is stored on your device
using the platform's secure-storage facility. Saved scenarios remain available
until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. The app does
not send these scenarios to the developer's servers.

## Device authentication

The app can ask Android to authenticate you with the device's configured
screen-lock method, which may include biometrics. Authentication is performed
by the operating system. The app receives the authentication result; it does
not receive or store your fingerprint, face data, PIN, or device password.

## Information sent outside the app

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or automatic crash-reporting
features. It does not automatically send loan details to the developer.

If you select the feedback option, the app opens an email application with the
developer's contact address. You decide whether to send the email and what to
include, including any loan details. Your email provider will handle the
message under its own privacy terms.

Android and services used to distribute the app or provide device
authentication may process information under their own terms and privacy
policies. The app does not control those services.

## Your choices and deleting information

- Delete saved scenarios from the app's **Saved Loans** screen.
- Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove app-stored scenarios and
  settings.
- Do not send a feedback email if you do not want its contents handled by your
  email provider and the recipient.

## Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose loan calculator and is not designed to collect
information from children. It does not ask users to provide an age.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated. The effective
date above indicates when this version took effect.
